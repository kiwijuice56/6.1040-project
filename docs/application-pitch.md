# Name
Sticky
## Motivation
Day-to-day tasks like boarding a train or booking a doctor's appointment might seem trivial, but can be overwhelming to inexperienced or anxiety-prone people, especially when the rules can vary from place to place.
## Key features
### Sticky noting
Whenever you need help getting through a daily task, look on the in-app map for explanatory sticky notes placed by other users. Other people like yourself who struggled with the same doctor's office or airport might have left anonymous tips on digital sticky notes tied to real locations.
