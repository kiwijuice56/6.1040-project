# App name: Sticky
## Motivation
Day-to-day tasks like boarding a train or booking a doctor's appointment might seem trivial, but can be overwhelming to inexperienced or anxiety-prone people, especially when the rules can vary from place to place.
## Key features
### Sticky noting
Whenever you need help getting through a daily task, look at the in-app map for explanatory sticky notes. Other people like yourself who struggled with the same doctor's office or airport might have left anonymous tips on digital sticky notes tied to real locations! Unlike the advice you might find on the internet, Sticky notes can provide context specific to the exact institution that you're navigating. Writing your own sticky notes can help both yourself and other people facing your same issue in the future.

### Searching
Sticky has a search bar for scrubbing through large collections of notes. Rather than wasting hours digging through forums or outdated websites to get your questions answered, you can distill all of the advice from other Sticky users at once.

(Note: this is likely the weakest feature at the moment, but I am considering augmenting it into an LLM chatbot. It would use the same infrastructure as the search engine, but it would be able to answer questions more flexibly and potentially use the internet to fill gaps in user notes. However, I'm mixed on incorporating an AI feature because it may dilute the human element of the app and provide inaccurate information)

### Saving
Forget keeping dozens of bookmarks and notes for your questions: save the sticky notes that you find most helpful to your collection so that you can come back to them any time you're struggling with the same task. You can also filter the sticky notes on the map to display only your saved notes, helping you create a clear mental picture of the exact steps relevant to you in completing a task without stress.
