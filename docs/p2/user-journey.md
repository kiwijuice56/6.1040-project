# Updated user journey
A student has recently been admitted to MIT and wishes to tour campus, but she is nervous about the particular details about being a visitor on campus (building access policy, unspoken rules at MIT, etc.). Her online searches returned sparse results for her worries, so she opens the Sticky app to find relevant and local advice. After navigating around the campus map and reading Sticky notes placed by other users (and upvoting helpful notes), she feels more knowledgable and confident about navigating campus.

Another helpful user struggled with similar worries in the past, so they use the Sticky app to write notes that both themselves and other Sticky users can refer to later.
