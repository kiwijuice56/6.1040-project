# Updated user journey
[Link to recording](https://youtu.be/mOpjAN1GwCs).

A student has recently been admitted to MIT and wishes to tour campus, but she is nervous about the particular details about being a visitor on campus (building access policy, unspoken rules at MIT, etc.). Her online searches returned sparse results for her worries, so she opens the Sticky app to find relevant and local advice. She navigates to MIT on the map and casually looks through the Sticky notes that were placed by other users. She also uses the search bar to look for notes relevant to her key questions. She skips over the notes with many downvotes, as she assumes they won't be useful. After reading through the notes and saving the most important ones to her collection, she feels more knowledgable and confident about navigating campus. 

Another helpful user struggled with similar worries in the past, so they use the Sticky app to write notes that both themselves and other Sticky users can refer to later.

*Note*: the video reverses the order in which the actions taken by both users are taken because it must assume the database is empty. I changed the order here to more clearly communicate the bad situation.
